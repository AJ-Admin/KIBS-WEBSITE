Admission Information

We welcome applications from students who are eager to learn and grow. Below are the basic requirements needed for enrollment processing.

📝 General Requirements

  • Completed application form profile
  • Copies of previous school report cards
  • 2 passport-sized photographs

📄 Additional Documents

  • Birth certificate or national ID
  • Medical clearance certificate
  • Proof of residence (utility bill or tenancy agreement)

School Fees Payment via Mobile Money

All school fees and registration balances must be cleared securely using either MTN MoMo or Airtel Money. Follow the exact step-by-step instructions below to complete your payment.

🟡 Method 1: MTN MoMo (SchoolPay)

Follow these steps to pay using your MTN line:

  1. Dial *165*4*3# on your MTN phone.
  2. Select Option 1 (SchoolPay).
  3. Enter the student's unique Student Code (provided on your admission or fees structure slip).
  4. Enter the exact Amount of school fees you wish to pay.
  5. Confirm the student's name appears correctly on your screen to avoid paying for the wrong account.
  6. Enter your MTN MoMo PIN to authorize the transaction.
  7. Wait for the confirmation SMS from MTN and SchoolPay before closing your screen. Keep this message as proof of payment.

🔴 Method 2: Airtel Money (SchoolPay)

Follow these steps to pay using your Airtel line:

  1. Dial *185*6# on your Airtel phone.
  2. Select Option 2 (School Fees) or choose SchoolPay if listed.
  3. Enter the school's merchant code or the unique Student Code.
  4. Enter the exact Amount you want to deposit for school fees.
  5. Carefully review the payment details on the screen, making sure the student's name matches your records.
  6. Enter your Airtel Money PIN to complete the transaction.
  7. You will receive a confirmation message from Airtel Money verifying the payment went through successfully.
⚠️ Please Note: Cash payments are strictly not accepted at the school administration premises for safety and tracking purposes. Always verify the student name on your phone screen before entering your secret PIN.

How to Apply

We welcome all prospective students to join our community at KIBS Primary School. To begin your application process or to learn more about our requirements, please follow these steps:

  • Visit Us: Come to our campus to pick up an application package and take a tour of our facilities.
  • Call Us: You can speak directly with our admissions office for guidance on the current enrollment status.

All contact information, including our physical location, office hours, and phone numbers, can be found on our Contact Us page.
